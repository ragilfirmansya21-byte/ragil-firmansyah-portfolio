Bachelor's Thesis · Dec 2025 – Jun 2026

Solar Dryer Performance Study

Mixed Solar Dryer Experimental Study · Effect of Collector Inclination Angle on Thermal and Drying Performance

Overview

Measured performance in a purpose-built dryer.

Designed and experimentally evaluated a mixed solar dryer to investigate the effect of collector inclination angle on thermal efficiency and drying performance during kemplang cracker drying.

My role

  • Led team task allocation, testing schedules, documentation, and project expenses.
  • Performed solar geometry calculations to support collector inclination variations.
  • Designed and assembled the dryer, conducted testing, and analysed thermal and drying data.

Results

Measured comparative performance

Among the tested collector inclination angles, one configuration showed the best overall balance between thermal efficiency and drying performance. A verified final angle and chart can be added when the thesis evidence is supplied.

Testing

Experimental data collection included direct measurements across thermal and drying parameters, with sample mass monitored during the drying process.

Methods

Methods

  • Solar geometry calculations
  • Collector inclination variation
  • Direct experimental measurements
  • Thermal efficiency analysis
  • Drying performance analysis

Measured parameters

Temperature · Air velocity · Relative humidity · Illuminance · Moisture content · Sample mass
