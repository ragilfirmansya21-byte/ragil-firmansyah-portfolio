MSIB CFD Learning Track Capstone Project · 2024

Helical VAWT CFD Study

Overview

Collaborative flow visualization.

Contributed to a five-member team project that used transient CFD analysis to visualize airflow and pressure distribution around a three-bladed helical vertical-axis wind turbine.

My role

  • Worked collaboratively with a five-member CFD project team.
  • Gained project-based exposure to simulation conditions, boundary definitions, and CFD post-processing.
  • Contributed to reviewing velocity, pressure, wake-flow results, and the final technical presentation.

Results

Local acceleration & wake formation

The simulation showed local airflow acceleration around rotating blades, uneven pressure distribution across blade surfaces, and wake formation downstream of the turbine.

CFD setup

Transient CFD

3D transient CFD · SST k–ω turbulence model · 10 m/s velocity inlet · Rotating domain · Pressure outlet · ANSYS Fluent

Scope note

Academic learning-track work. It does not claim turbine optimization, experimental validation, validated aerodynamic performance, or advanced CFD expertise.
