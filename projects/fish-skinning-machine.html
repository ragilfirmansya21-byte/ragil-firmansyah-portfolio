Capstone Design Project · Oct 2025 – Dec 2025

Fish Skinning Machine Prototype

Overview

Early-stage machine development.

Led a team project to develop a low-cost semi-automatic fish skinning machine prototype as a more affordable option for small-scale fish processing businesses.

My role

  • Coordinated task allocation, schedule, procurement, documentation, and project expenses.
  • Contributed to the concept and selection of available components based on function, availability, and budget.
  • Worked collaboratively in fabrication and assembly, including shaft turning and knurling, grinding, welding, drilling, and hand tools.

Test results

Mechanism issues documented

Initial testing provided an early evaluation of the mechanism. The fish sample was damaged during testing, with blade sharpness and pressing control identified as possible areas for improvement.

Next improvements

Sharper food-grade blade, adjustable pressing mechanism, improved feeding and guiding system, and additional prototype testing.

Fabrication

Hands-on work

Conventional lathe · Shaft turning · Knurling · Grinding · Welding · Drilling · Hand tools · Prototype assembly

Project status

Early-stage prototype development. Initial testing only; no claims of commercial operation, validated production speed, or industry-ready performance are made.
