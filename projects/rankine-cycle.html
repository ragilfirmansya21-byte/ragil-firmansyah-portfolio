Double-Reheat Rankine Cycle

Regenerative Rankine Cycle Analysis · Conceptual steam power cycle with two open feedwater heaters

Overview

Calculated under selected assumptions.

Developed and analyzed a conceptual regenerative Rankine cycle with two reheaters and two open feedwater heaters, evaluating its ability to meet project targets of at least 250 MW net power and more than 38% thermal efficiency.

Results

Calculated academic results

Under the selected assumptions, the cycle was calculated to produce approximately 271.2 MW of net power with thermal efficiency of approximately 45.9%, exceeding the academic project targets.
